Blocked by macOS the first time? That’s expected
  1. Double-click Swaylume and let it be refused once. Don’t skip this — macOS has to record the block before the button below appears.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to Security and find the line saying Swaylume was blocked.
  4. Click Open Anyway next to it, then confirm.

After that it opens with a normal double-click and won’t ask again. Swaylume isn’t notarized by Apple yet (that requires a paid developer account) — it doesn’t mean anything is wrong with the app.
